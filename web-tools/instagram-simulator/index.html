About Instagram Simulator

👋 Welcome! This is a fun, single-file Instagram profile simulator (light mode).

How to use it:

  • Tap the + in the top bar to create a new post
  • Tap any post in the grid to view it full-screen (double-tap the photo to like)
  • Tap & hold a post then drag to reorder your feed
  • Tap Edit profile to change name, username, bio, followers, and avatar
  • Tap any highlight circle to edit or add new ones
  • Everything is saved automatically in your browser

Just for fun — preview your profile before you post on real Instagram! 📸
